Step 1 of 4

Which lift?

One lift per clip. A clean & jerk is scored as two: the clean, then the jerk.

Step 2 of 4

Film it like this

  1. 1Square to the side. Not in front, not at an angle — drift is measured sideways.
  2. 23–5 m away, camera at hip height.
  3. 3Landscape. Portrait works but lowers confidence.
  4. 4Whole bar and both plates in frame for the entire lift.
  5. 5One lift per clip. You can trim it on the next screen.

Result

Scores are indicative. They read bar path from sampled frames, not from pose tracking, and a clip that is angled, dark or holds more than one lift lowers the confidence above.
